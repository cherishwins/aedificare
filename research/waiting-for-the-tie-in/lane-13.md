Using the post-Q2 figures the sum is over 700 at every end of the range; the cited April source gives about 700 and was a plan. Cited source 34 was not opened for this claim. A minimal fix is to attribute "over 700 billion" to a dated sum of the four companies' updated guidance, with the definitions noted.
