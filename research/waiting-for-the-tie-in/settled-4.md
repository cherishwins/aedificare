## For the owner

- **"The rest of the country is slower."** LBNL counts generation and storage only, and ERCOT's generation queue is part of its count. The sentence follows ERCOT's 474 GW of load requests and its "about 20 months" for a load tie-in. So it sets a generation median against a load wait, and the "rest of the country" figure includes Texas. Each number is true. Whether the comparison stands, or the sentence should read something like "Power plants wait longer still", is the author's call.
- **"to hold a place in line"** (4.6) is the author's inference under the I mark, and no source here states that motive. Keep it as the author's reading or drop it.
- **The sliders' captions are stocks, not rates.** The sliders are "a year", but Dominion's 9.4 GW and 4.8 GW and AEP's 69 GW are a contract stock, a billing peak and a five-year total. The captions are now true as notes. Whether they belong beside annual rates is the owner's call on the model.
- **The print and the carousel** (public/pdf/edition-08.pdf) carry the old wording and are left as issued, as every ported edition's print is. Slide 04 has "Virginia: up to seven." with no year, and "5+ YEARS. Median wait for projects built in 2025." without LBNL's "regions with available data".
