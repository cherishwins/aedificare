Notes on the settlement.

- 25.1: the label "Engineering bachelor's degrees" holds for NCES's 126,037 and not for its 145,041, so the smaller true change is the US cell, not the label (relabelling would also redefine the unverified China cell). The bar value moves from 145 to 126.
- 25.2: the 54% has no primary; NSF's own survey gives 55.7% for engineering in 2023, printed as 56% with its year. The skeptic's inference that 54% may be mathematics and statistics (53.2% in 2023) is not printed. "~77k" and "~40k" stay: CSET says "more than 77,000" and "approximately 40,000", both projections, which the note now dates.
- 25.3: "all trades" became "all occupations": DOL's completer count is every registered apprenticeship, not trades alone. "All vocational graduates" was the edition's gloss on "technical personnel".
- 25.4: "After the $100,000 fee" is dropped because in a side-by-side row it reads as the cause, and no source opened attributes the fall to the fee; USCIS's own table shows duplicates were already out of the FY2026 base (7,828), so the researcher's duplicates explanation is not printed either. The researcher's Clearinghouse "quote" carried the researcher's own words inside the marks and is not used. The −38.5% is 211,600 against 343,981 (arithmetic holds), but 211,600 appears on no USCIS page and Fragomen could not be opened; against total registrations the fall would be −41.0%, against unique beneficiaries −37.6%.
