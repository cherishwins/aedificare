## 25.4. [08 · Side by side (Minerals and people)] H-1B registrations, FY2027 change | n/a | −38.5% | Fragomen. After the $100,000 fee, since vacated in one circuit. (attributed to Fragomen) (cites 77)
Verdict: wrong
Primary: Fragomen, "United States H-1B cap is reached for FY 2027" (insight dated 17 July 2026, per secondary reports). https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-h-1b-cap-is-reached-for-fy-2027.html (page not opened: HTTP 403). No. 4:25-cv-08454, per Clearinghouse case page https://clearinghouse.net/case/47032/ ; Ogletree, "Federal Court Issues New Order Blocking Agency Implementation of $100,000 H-1B Fee", 2 Oct 2026, https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/federal-court-issues-new-order-blocking-agency-implementation-of-100000-h-1b-fee
Read: 2026-11-01
Quote: Clearinghouse: "Sept. 30, 2026: The court granted in part and denied in part the plaintiffs' motion for a preliminary injunction. The order vacated the agency's policies implementing the $100,000 fee, but the page does not describe it as vacating the proclamation itself." Ogletree: the court "blocked federal agencies from enforcing or implementing policies tied to the $100,000 H-1B payment requirement."
True as: USCIS counted 211,600 properly submitted FY2027 H-1B registrations against 343,981 for FY2026, a fall of about 38.5% (checked: 211,600 / 343,981 − 1 = −38.5%). The $100,000 fee has been challenged in several courts: a Massachusetts ruling of 8 June 2026 and a California order of 30 September 2026 each blocked it in part, while a D.C. court upheld it in December 2025. Its status is contested and under appeal.
Notes: (1) The Fragomen page returned HTTP 403 and was not read, so I cannot confirm that Fragomen gives the −38.5% figure. Secondary search snippets say USCIS data gave 211,600 against 343,981, roughly 38%. (2) "Vacated in one circuit" is wrong or incomplete on the record found: a Massachusetts decision (First Circuit) and an N.D. Cal. order (Ninth Circuit) both blocked the fee, and Proclamation 11069, issued 18 Sept 2026, extends it through 21 Sept 2027 (Clearinghouse). Reports of the Massachusetts order's status conflict (one says the First Circuit denied a stay on 24 July 2026; another says the order was paused pending appeal). I did not open the court dockets. (3) The 38.5% fall is a registration count, not a selection or cap-filing figure. (4) Causation: "After the $100,000 fee" is a sequence, not a cause. Secondary commentary attributes the drop partly to USCIS's effort to cut duplicate registrations and to the new weighted selection rule. Do not state the fee as the cause.
