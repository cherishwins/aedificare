## 9.1. Since 26 Aug 2026 an executive order bars Chinese bulk-power equipment from new transactions (EO 14421; cites 18)
Ruling: **amended** (the verdict "overstated" and the True as stand. The Primary line and notes (3) and (5) change, because the Federal Register text is now read.)
Evidence:
- Federal Register, official GPO PDF, Vol. 91, No. 167, Monday, August 31, 2026, pp. 55995-55999, FR Doc. 2026-17843, "Executive Order 14421 of August 26, 2026: Declaring a National Emergency To Secure the United States Bulk-Power System". https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-08-31/pdf/2026-17843.pdf
  - Sec. 2(a): "The following actions are prohibited: any acquisition, importation, transfer, or installation of any foreign-produced bulk-power system electric equipment (transaction) by any person, or with respect to any property, subject to the jurisdiction of the United States, where the transaction involves any property in which any foreign country or a national thereof has any interest ..., where the transaction was initiated after the date of this order, and where the Secretary of Energy (Secretary) ... has determined that: (i) the transaction involves bulk-power system electric equipment ... designed, developed, manufactured, or supplied by persons owned by, controlled by, or subject to the jurisdiction or direction of a Covered Foreign Entity; and (ii) the transaction: (A) poses an undue risk ..."
  - Sec. 2(b): "Upon making the determinations described in subsection (a) of this section, the Secretary ... may impose conditions on the continued use, operation, maintenance, servicing, or updating of foreign manufactured or operated bulk-power system electric equipment acquired or installed before the date of this order, including requirements to identify, isolate, monitor, secure, disconnect, replace, or remove such equipment."
  - Sec. 3(b): "Within 120 days of the date of this order, the Secretary ... shall publish rules or regulations implementing the authorities delegated to the Secretary by this order as needed." The same section says rules "may, among other things, determine that particular countries or persons are a Covered Foreign Entity exclusively for the purposes of this order".
  - Sec. 5(a): the bulk-power system "includes transmission lines rated at 69,000 volts (69 kV) or more, but does not include facilities used in the local distribution of electric energy."
  - Sec. 5(e): "The term ''Covered Foreign Entity'' means a country or any person owned by, controlled by, or subject to the jurisdiction or direction of a government of a foreign country that is subject to a United States arms embargo or sanctions regime, under the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (22 C.F.R. 126.1), or that the Secretary ... has determined is engaged in conduct that is detrimental to the national security or foreign policy of the United States."
  - No country is named anywhere in the order's five pages.
- White House page (URL in the lane file): signed August 26, 2026, number 14421. The quoted Sec. 2(a) fragments match the Federal Register text.
- DOE, https://www.energy.gov/node/4860229 : "Executive Order 14421"; "The executive order authorizes the U.S. Secretary of Energy to prohibit certain acquisitions, imports, transfers," (verbatim to that point). "DOE will move quickly to execute the executive order and issue implementing rules within 120 days." The only DOE action the page reports is a request for information: "The department issued a request for information (RFI)."
- Orrick, dated "September.01.2026", URL in the lane file: "The executive order imposes no immediate obligations on private parties"; "Countries currently covered by the definition include, most notably, China and Russia."; "issue implementing regulations by December 24, 2026". The page links the govinfo PDF above.
Problem: (1) Note (5) says the order's Federal Register text was not opened and 91 FR 55995 (31 Aug 2026) was unverified. Both are now confirmed from the official text, FR Doc. 2026-17843, 91 FR 55995-55999. The quotes the researcher took from the White House fetch match it. (2) Note (3) is right that the order names no country. "Chinese" rests on the order's cross-reference to 22 C.F.R. 126.1, as Orrick reads it. I could not open 126.1 itself (ecfr.gov redirects to the unblock host, not followed) and could not search for another copy. (3) Note (6) can now be partly answered. DOE's own page reports an RFI and promises rules within 120 days (by 24 Dec 2026 per Orrick), and reports no determination. That this absence still holds on 1 Nov 2026 is untested: I could not search. (4) Scope the researcher did not record: the order covers only bulk-power system equipment, meaning transmission at 69 kV or more, and excludes local distribution. (5) The chain variant, "Chinese units barred since Aug 2026.", has the same problem and needs the same fix.
What the page should say instead: the True as stays. The Primary line should read: "Executive Order 14421 of August 26, 2026, 91 FR 55995 (31 Aug 2026), FR Doc. 2026-17843 (official GPO text, govinfo.gov); White House and DOE pages as cross-checks." Note (5) should read: "Federal Register text read: 91 FR 55995-55999; the White House quotes match it." Note (3) should add: "The order defines Covered Foreign Entity by reference to the ITAR arms-embargo list, 22 C.F.R. 126.1, plus countries the Secretary designates; 126.1 itself was not opened." The chain line should read, for example: "Covered-country units can be barred since Aug 2026, once DOE rules."
