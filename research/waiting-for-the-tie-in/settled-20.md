## For the owner

- **20.6, a choice of row.** The settled row keeps Oenergetice's $497 and $1,371 with its source named, because that is what the edition printed and it is truly Oenergetice's. But Oenergetice names no source for them, and its other figures disagree with IRENA (a global $747/kW and -11% against IRENA's $667/kW and -6%; a China LCOE of $21/MWh against IRENA's $36). IRENA's full report, which might give country installed costs, sat behind a 403 (its publication page) and was not reached.
- **The print, page 13, is left as issued and carries three of the faults fixed on the page**: "540" without the tilde (a derived figure read as stated), "Grid storage" and "16" for the US, which rounds an unsourced 15.8 up and away from EIA's 15.

The row sets China's all-sources total beside it; read FERC on a normal connection if you want "Not like for like" on this row too.
- **20.5, the date.** The WNA page both agents read was updated 23 Oct 2026, eight days after the edition's date; the version the edition used was not seen. The counts may well be the same. WNA's count also includes some suspended units.
- **20.1, coal.** "to 95" is dropped because no one opened Carbon Brief's page; if you want the range back, it needs that page read.
