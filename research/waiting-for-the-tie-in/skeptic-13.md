## 13.2. Amazon trailing-twelve-month free cash flow minus 7.6 billion
Verdict: upheld (researcher: holds), with a better primary and the definition the researcher left unchecked
Evidence:
- Cited source 35, Amazon release, https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-earnings-q2-2026-report : "Free cash flow decreased to an outflow of $7.6 billion for the trailing twelve months", driven by "a year-over-year increase of $66.1 billion in purchases of property and equipment, net of proceeds from sales and incentives"; "This compares to free cash flow inflow of $18.2 billion for the trailing twelve months ended June 30, 2025." The quote is verbatim.
- Better primary: Amazon Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1, filed 30 July 2026 (accession 0001018724-26-000024), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000024/amzn-20260630xex991.htm . Supplemental table: "Operating cash flow -- trailing twelve months (TTM)" $161,403; "Purchases of property and equipment, net of proceeds from sales and incentives -- TTM" $169,007; "Free cash flow -- TTM (1)" $(7,604). Footnote (1): "Free cash flow is cash flow from operations reduced by" "Purchases of property and equipment, net of proceeds from sales and incentives."
Scope: the figure is for the twelve months to 30 June 2026, on Amazon's own definition, which nets out proceeds and incentives and does not deduct finance-lease principal. The researcher's "true as" is accurate.
Correction to the researcher's note: "The release gives no quarterly free cash flow figure" is true of the web release, but the SEC exhibit's cash-flow statement gives the quarter's components. For the three months to 30 June 2026: "Net cash provided by (used in) operating activities" 45,387; "Purchases of property and equipment" (54,208); "Proceeds from property and equipment sales and incentives" 1,132. On Amazon's definition that is 45,387 - (54,208 - 1,132) = about -7.7 billion for the quarter (my arithmetic; Amazon does not print a quarterly free-cash-flow line). This matters for 13.4.
Should say: no change to the edition's sentence.
