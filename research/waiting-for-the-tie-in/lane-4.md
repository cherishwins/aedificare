The release also mentions "fully executed take-or-pay electric service agreements" under a separate heading (context not confirmed). Figures such as 45 GW for Texas and 12 GW for Ohio came from a secondary summary and are not used here. Search results mention ERCOT review as a timing risk, but the release text did not address timing.
