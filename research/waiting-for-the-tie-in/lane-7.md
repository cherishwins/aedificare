## 7.1. Wood Mackenzie counted about 110 gigawatts on order at the end of 2025 against 60 to 70 gigawatts of global factory capacity a year, with prices heading for roughly 195 percent above 2019. (attributed to Wood Mackenzie) (cites 13)
Verdict: overstated
Primary: Wood Mackenzie, "Gas turbine prices soar 195% as market faces supply-demand crisis", 1 April 2026. https://www.woodmac.com/press-releases/gas-turbine-prices-soar-195-as-market-faces-supply-demand-crisis/
Read: 2026-11-01
Quote: "global orders sat at 110 gigawatts (GW) at the end of 2025, but global manufacturing capacity is only capable of 60-70 GW." / "This has pushed prices to new highs, with the market anticipated to reach US$600/kW by end-2027—a 195% increase since 2019."
True as: Wood Mackenzie said global orders sat at 110 GW at the end of 2025, against global manufacturing capacity of 60 to 70 GW, and expects turbine prices to reach US$600/kW by end-2027, a 195% rise on 2019.
Notes: The release does not say "a year" for the 60 to 70 GW; the unit is not stated. Drop "a year" unless a full-report page supports it. The release attributes the figures to "the report" whose title begins "The US gas turbine market", but the sentence labels them global. I did not open the full report, so I cannot say whether the global scope is the report's own. Figures are as published, not checked against the full report.
