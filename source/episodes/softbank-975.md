## Cold open

[[footage#sb-tokyo: Tokyo at night from above, the Shiodome towers where SoftBank has its headquarters | find: NASA ISS night photograph of Tokyo (public domain), or Wikimedia Commons "Shiodome" at night]] On the first of October, SoftBank pays OpenAI 10 billion dollars. [[footage#son-roosevelt: the Stargate announcement in the Roosevelt Room, wide, Son, Altman and Ellison at the lectern | find: the same White House video, a wide moment, silent]] Masayoshi Son likes a round number. ## The bond

[[footage#money-press: US currency coming off a printing press, sheets of notes | find: Bureau of Engraving and Printing B-roll (public domain), or DVIDS "currency printing"]] On the twenty-fourth of September, SoftBank priced about 11.1 billion dollars of bonds, in dollars and euros. [s1] The longest pays 9.75% a year until 2034. [s1] [[over: 9.75% · 2034 | USD 4.5B · 7.5 YEARS · NO COLLATERAL]] Reuters, citing LSEG, called it the largest high-yield corporate bond sale on record. [s7] [[gag#sandow-flex: Eugen Sandow flexing, the Edison film of 1894 | find: Library of Congress "Sandow" 1894 (public domain)]] High-yield is the polite word for junk. S&P and Fitch both rate these notes double-B-plus. [s1]

[[quote: s7 | "Now AI-driven debt issuance has reached the high-yield market at scale." | SATORU AOYAMA, SENIOR DIRECTOR, FITCH RATINGS | hl: reached the high-yield market at scale]] Fitch's Satoru Aoyama said he was positively surprised by the appetite. [[chart: range | Every sale has cost more than the last | sub: SOFTBANK DOLLAR AND EURO BOND SALES · LOWEST TO HIGHEST COUPON | unit: % | src: s7, s6, s1 | Jun 2021=2.125..5.25 | Apr 2026=6.375..8.5 | Sep 2026=7.125..9.75*]] In April, SoftBank had borrowed for ten years at 8.5%. [s6] Five months later it paid 9.75% to borrow for seven and a half. [s1, s6] [[chart: bars | The price of not being a country | sub: ANNUAL COUPON OR YIELD · 24 SEPTEMBER 2026 | unit: % | src: s11, s1 | US Treasury, 7 years=5.10 | SoftBank dollar notes, 7.5 years=9.75*]] On the same day, seven-year United States Treasuries yielded 5.10%. [s11] The gap is the price of not being a country.

[s5] Japan Credit Rating Agency rates it A. [s5] Every buyer receives the Otosan Cheer Captain Side Pocket Tote Bag. [[quote: s5 | "Shipping will begin sequentially in late February 2027. Please look forward to it." | SOFTBANK GROUP, 4 SEP 2026 | hl: Please look forward to it.]] Shipping begins in February 2027. [s5]

## What backs it

[[footage#server-hall: rows of supercomputer racks, blinking | find: NASA Ames Pleiades supercomputer B-roll (public domain)]] For its 64.6 billion dollars, SoftBank holds preferred shares in OpenAI that turn into ordinary shares upon an IPO. [s2] On the twelfth of September, Sam Altman was asked whether that would happen this year. [s8] [[quote: s8 | "I would say not 2026." | SAM ALTMAN, CEO, OPENAI, 12 SEP 2026 | hl: not 2026]] The bond was priced twelve days later.

[[quote: s7 | "Risks for SoftBank credit are material and have increased as concentration has increased and cash flow has come under material strain." | MARK CHAPMAN, CREDITSIGHTS | hl: cash flow has come under material strain]] CreditSights' Mark Chapman calculates that Arm and OpenAI are now three-quarters of SoftBank's asset value. [s7] [[chart: bars | Insuring against a SoftBank default got dearer | sub: 5-YEAR CREDIT DEFAULT SWAP SPREAD · BASIS POINTS A YEAR | src: s7 | June 2026, about=280 | Week of the sale=400 (400+)*]] In the week of the sale, insuring SoftBank's debt against default cost more than 400 basis points a year, up from about 280 in June. [s7]

## The circle

[[map: Tokyo 35.68,139.69 > San Francisco 37.77,-122.42 > Piketon 39.07,-83.01 | TOKYO · SAN FRANCISCO · PIKE COUNTY, OHIO]] SoftBank also controls SB Energy, which filed to go public on the twenty-first of September. [s9] In August, SB Energy leased a data centre site in Pike County, Ohio, to OpenAI, for 20 years. [s9] [[quote: s9 | "In August 2026, we leased the data center site to OpenAI, a strategic investor in our business, under a 20-year term." | SB ENERGY, SEC FORM S-1/A, 21 SEP 2026 | hl: a strategic investor in our business]] OpenAI, the filing adds, has invested in SB Energy. [[chart: flow | The money goes round | src: s2, s9 | SoftBank > OpenAI=$30B* | SoftBank > SB Energy=controls | OpenAI > SB Energy=invested | SB Energy > OpenAI=20-year lease]] So SoftBank's money goes to OpenAI, which rents land from a company SoftBank controls, which OpenAI has invested in. [[footage#piketon-site: the Portsmouth gaseous diffusion plant at Piketon, Ohio, from the air | find: energy.gov/pppo photo and video galleries (public domain), or a Landsat frame of Piketon]] It sits on the Energy Department's old Portsmouth site: more than 3,700 acres that held a gaseous diffusion plant and a depleted uranium conversion facility. [s9, s10] The plan is 17 buildings and 8 gigawatts of computing. [s9]

[[quote: s9 | "No data center capacity is currently in operation." | SB ENERGY, SEC FORM S-1/A, 21 SEP 2026 | hl: No data center capacity is currently in operation.]] In the first half of 2026, SB Energy booked 138.7 million dollars of revenue and a net loss of 3.19 billion dollars, most of it an accounting charge on warrants. [s9]

## The number

[[chart: stack | $941.25 million a year in coupons | sub: DOLLAR NOTES ONLY · OUR ARITHMETIC FROM THE TERMS | prefix: $ | unit: M | src: s1 | Due 2030, 8.625% on $1B=86.25 | Due 2032, 9.25% on $4.5B=416.25 | Due 2034, 9.75% on $4.5B=438.75*]] Add up the coupons on the dollar notes and SoftBank owes its new lenders 941.25 million dollars a year. [s1] That is about 2.58 million dollars a day, by our arithmetic from its own terms. [s1] [[footage#sb-tokyo-2: Tokyo at night again, a slower shot | find: the same source as sb-tokyo, a different moment]] Interest is due every first of April and first of October, until 2034. [s1] The payment to OpenAI is due on the first of October. [s1] It is a busy date.
