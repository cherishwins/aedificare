## The lease

[[footage#georgia-lines: high-voltage transmission lines crossing Georgia pine country | find: Library of Congress, Carol M. Highsmith Archive, search "Georgia" and "power lines" (public domain); or the US Department of Energy photo library at energy.gov]] CleanSpark has mined bitcoin at Sandersville, Georgia, since 2022. [s7, s8] On the tenth of July, it signed a 20-year lease there for 175 megawatts of data centre capacity. [s6] The site is about 122 acres of newly bought land, next to the mine. [s4, s8] The lease is triple net: the tenant pays every cost, down to the indemnities. [s6]

[[over: $6.6B · 20 YEARS | RENT OVER THE FIRST TERM · ESCALATING 3.0% A YEAR]] Over the first 20 years, the rent comes to about 6.6 billion dollars, rising 3.0% a year. [s7, s8] In July, CleanSpark would say only that the tenant was a high-investment-grade global technology company. [s7] [[fig: ANVIRAN, LLC | THE TENANT · A WHOLLY OWNED SUBSIDIARY OF META]] The name arrived in September, on a slide for bond investors. [s1] The tenant is Anviran, LLC, a subsidiary of Meta, and Meta Platforms is its guarantor. [s1, s4]
