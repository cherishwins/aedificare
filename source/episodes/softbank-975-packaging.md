# Episode 1 packaging: SoftBank, 4.75% and 9.75%

Titles and thumbnails for YouTube's Test & Compare, for `softbank-975.md`
(the script's front matter already carries all three of each, so the render
writes `thumb.png`, `thumb-b.png` and `thumb-c.png` and lists them in
`sheet.txt`). Written 2026-09-26 from the channel brief, sections 3, 4, 5
and 8, and the three judges' notes. Every claim in a title or thumbnail is
said in the film, with its source.

## Titles

B and C under 70 characters, A at 75; numerals, and either the contrast or a phrase
people type.

| | Title | Characters | Why it might win |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | SoftBank Pays Yen Savers 4.75% and a Tote Bag. Its Dollars Cost Up to 9.75% | 75 | Both numbers and the contrast in one line, each with its currency (the fact-check's point: a yen coupon beside a dollar coupon with no currency is the mixed comparison the brief retired) and "up to", because only 4.5 of the 10 billion pays 9.75%. | C | An AI Circular Deal, Financed at Up to 9.75% | 44 | The searched phrase ("ai circular deals"), short enough that the Suggested sidebar shows all of it; paid off by the chapter of that name at about 6:20. |

not argue a bubble, and it is SoftBank that borrows, so the title would be

## Notes for the owner

- The live lookalike (brief 8.1): @TheComputeBill's "SoftBank Borrowed
  $11.1 Billion at Up to 9.75% to Pay OpenAI". None of these titles opens
  with that formula. If that channel is not yours, keep it that way.
- The Short: `short: Cold open, Where 9.75% sits` cuts about 1:55 from the
  film, ending on the ladder. The ladder has four rungs, and the render warns
  past three in a Short. A 30 to 45 second Short built for the format needs
  its own script: a dollar-only ladder of three rungs (7-year Treasury 5.05 on 23 Sep,
  SoftBank due 2030 at 8.625, due 2034 at 9.75), with the yen bond and the
  bag kept in words ("a different currency"), never on the same axis.
- The description's first paragraph (the `hook:`) ends by saying the voice
  is synthetic, as brief 8.11 asks.
