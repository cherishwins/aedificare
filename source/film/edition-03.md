## 00 The number

In February 2024 the United States Air Force paid 5.1 million dollars for 280 mesh radios and ten antennas. That is 18,214 dollars a radio, with the antennas thrown in, which was generous of them.

Seven years earlier the Army National Guard bought 950 of the same radio for 8.9 million dollars. Under 9,400 each. The price roughly doubled. The radio did not.

The company that sells a build guide for the other kind of radio says this class of capability costs twenty thousand a unit. The public record says between nine and eighteen. A small discrepancy, unless you are the one paying.

The other kind of radio is a Raspberry Pi 5, a Morse Micro HaLow chip, an antenna, a battery hat and a box that came out of a printer. The guide prices it at 208 to 450 dollars a node. A network needs two, because a network of one is a monologue. The guide itself is 97 dollars, which makes it the most expensive item on the table until you reread the first paragraph.

Two nodes: four hundred and sixteen dollars at the floor. One government radio: 18,214. Same physics in both.
