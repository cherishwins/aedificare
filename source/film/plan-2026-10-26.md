## What Fireship-grade means here

A faceless channel with one synthetic voice cannot copy Jeff Delaney's
persona, his speed to the news, his demos or his team. It can copy the
grammar:

- 4 to 6 minutes, 550 to 1,300 words, written for the screen from the
  edition, never the edition read aloud;
- the subject in the title said in the first sentence, the first
  surprising number inside 10 seconds, the title card at 15 to 20
  percent of the runtime;
- 160 words a minute to start, toward 200; at most 4 pauses over 0.35 s
  a minute. The channel's voice is designed "unhurried" and ElevenLabs'
  speed tops out at 1.2, so most of the gain is in the writing;
- the rose alone on screen 10 percent of the time or less; about 19
  cuts a minute; nothing standing still past 3 s unless it is building;
  most pictures are evidence (the document, the chart, the mechanism),
  not the spoken words typed out;
- a dry line every 20 to 30 seconds; the record line and the dove kept
  short at the end; Shorts end on the payoff.

Every render is scored against those automatically once
`tools/film-score.py` exists (the audit's script, to be committed).

## Risks

- **YouTube's inauthentic-content policy** (July 2025) forbids templated
  AI content that looks mass-produced, readings of material you did not
  write, and slideshows with little narrative; it allows a fixed intro
  and outro with different substance each time. An original script per
  story with story-specific evidence on screen is the mitigation. Sixteen
  of the hundred biggest "AI slop" channels were removed by January 2026.
- **Looking like a knockoff.** Copy the grammar, never Fireship's
  thumbnails, sign-off, phrases or voice.
- **Content ID** on memes and clips; public-domain footage avoids most
  of it.
- **The humour**: much of Fireship's is developer in-jokes; the dry line
  travels better than the reference.
- **Brand rules that clash with the Fireship style**, each the owner's:
  written-in pauses against density; one voice against a sceptic; no
  "I"; only 120 or 900 ms, nothing eases (no glitch cuts, no eased
  zooms); Acid once per shot; nothing drawn (no hand-drawn arrows, no
  stickers, no cut-outs); no third typeface (meme captions in
  Bricolage); radius 2 px and no shadow; no call to action and no
  sponsor slot; the dove last.
